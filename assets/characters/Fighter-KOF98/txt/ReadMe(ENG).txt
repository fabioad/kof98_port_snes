=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Knees of Hell - F, y

Dragon Suplex - F, b


-- Command Moves
Shin Kyaku - F, x

Low Kick - F, a

Assassin's Blade - (in Front Jump)D, y


-- Super Moves
Open Fire - D, DF, F, x or y

Firespout - F, D, DF, x or y

Relentless Assault - D, DB, B, x or y(3 times)

Distortion Strike - (close)F, B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y

Mirror Sheen - D, DF, F, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Dragonstorm - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Galestrike - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b

Tempest of Light - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Dragonstorm - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Galestrike - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b

Tempest of Light - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y
