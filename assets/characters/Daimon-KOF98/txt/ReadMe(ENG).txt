Goro Daimon(KOF'98 Style) by Ikaruga
for WinMugen / Mugen 1.0

*Open Source

=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


-- Throw
Juuji Gatame - (near)F or B, y

Tsukami Tatakitsuke - (far)F or B, y

Okuri Ashibarai - F or B, b


-- Command Moves
Tama Tsubushi - F, x

Zujou Barai - DF, y


-- Super Moves
Jirai Shin - F, D, DF, x or y

Chou Ukemi - D, DB, B, a or b

Kumo Tsukami Nage - B, DB, D, DF, F, x

Kirikabu Gaeshi - B, DB, D, DF, F, y

Tenchi Gaeshi - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, x or y

Chou Ohsoto Gari - F, D, DF, a or b

Nekko Gaeshi - D, DF, F, a or b

Ura Nage - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Jigoku Gokuraku Otoshi - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y

Arashi no Yama           - B, DB, D, DF, F, B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b
 ->Zoku: Kirikabu Gaeshi - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b
  ->Bukkonuki Uranage    - F, D, DF, a or b


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Jigoku Gokuraku Otoshi - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y

Arashi no Yama           - B, DB, D, DF, F, B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b
 ->Zoku: Kirikabu Gaeshi - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b
  ->Zoku: Tenchi Gaeshi  - F, D, DF, a or b
