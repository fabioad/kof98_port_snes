Joe Higashi(KOF'98 Style) by Ikaruga
for WinMugen / Mugen 1.0

*Open Source

=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Hiza Jigoku - F, y

Leg Throw - F, b


-- Command Moves
Low Kick - F, a

Sliding - DF, a


-- Super Moves
Hurricane Upper - B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y

Bakuretsu Ken       - Tap a or x or y rapidly
 ->Bakuretsu Finish - D, DF, F, x or y

Tiger Kick - F, D, DF, a or b

Slash Kick - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b

Ougon no Kakato - D, DB, B, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Screw Upper - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Bakuretsu Hurricane Tiger Kakato - D, DF, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Screw Upper - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Bakuretsu Hurricane Tiger Kakato - D, DF, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y



*Fatal Fury Mode(select to start + any key)

-- Throw
Hiza Jigoku - F, y

Leg Throw - F, b


-- Command Moves
Low Kick - F, a

Sliding - DF, a


-- Super Moves
Hurricane Upper - B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y

Bakuretsu Ken       - Tap a or x or y rapidly
 ->Bakuretsu Finish - D, DF, F, x or y

Tiger Kick - F, D, DF, a or b

Slash Kick - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b

Pressure Knee          - F, D, DF, x or y
 ->Ougon no Fukurahagi - D, DB, B, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Screw Upper - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Thunder Fire - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Screw Upper - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Thunder Fire - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b
