Q&A

Q. What game is he based off?
A. Main base is 2002. She has many (but not all) moves from 94 - XI 

System:

Roll: (can be done whilst guarding, costs 1000 power): F or B a+b or c
Knockdown Attack: (Air)(can be done whilst guarding, costs 1000 power): b+y or z
Super Cancel(SC): Perform a desperation move command during certain special moves.
Dream Cancel(DC) Perform a super desperation move command during certain
desperation moves.  

Moves 

Throws

Shiranui Gourin: F + Y
Fuusha Kuzushi: F + B
Yume Zakura:  F+Y/B in air  

Command Moves

Dairin Fuusha Otoshi: D + x in air
Ukihane: D + a in air
Yusura Ume: D + b in air
Benitsuru no Mai: F + a
Koku'en no Mai: DF + a
Sankaku Tobi: UF or UB against the back/forward wall

Special Moves

Kachou Sen: D,DF,F x/y
Hissatsu Shinobi Bachi: B,DB,D,DF,F x/y
Ryuu Enbu: D,DB,B x/y (can be performed after Sayo Chidori) (SC)
Sayo Chidori: D,DB,B a/b
Musasabi no Mai (Kuuchuu): D,DB,B x/y in air
Hishou Ryuu'en Jin: F,D,DF a/b
Hakuro no Mai:  F,D,DF x/y  

Musasabi no Mai (Chijou)*: Charge d,u x/y                 
                           *Musasabi no Mai (Kuuchuu): hold x/y
                           *Musasabi no Mai (Kuuchuu): D,DB,B x/y in air
                           *Yusura Ume: D + x/y
                           *Ukihane: D + a/b
                           *Toki Tsubute: b+y or z when close 

Deperation Moves (1000 power required)
						   
Hana Arashi: D,DF,F,D,DF,F x/y
Chou Hissatsu Shinobi Bachi: D,DB,B,DB,D,DF,F a/b (DC into Gekka Botan only)

Super Desperation Moves (2000 power required)

Chou Hissatsu Shinobi Bachi: D,DB,B,DB,D,DF,F a+b
Leotard Shinobi Bachi: ???
Gekka Botan: D,DF,F,D,DF,F z in air

Hidden Super Desperation Moves (3000 power required)

Shiranui-ryuu: Kubi no Kitsune: D,DB,B,DB,D,DF,F x+y
