Ryuji Yamazaki(KOF'98 Style) by Ikaruga
for WinMugen / Mugen 1.0

*Open Source

=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Shime Age - F or B, y

Bun Nage - F or B, b


-- Command Moves
Bussashi - F, x


-- Super Moves
Hebi Tsukai: Upper - D, DB, B, x

Hebi Tsukai: Middle - D, DB, B, a

Hebi Tsukai: Lower - D, DB, B, y

Sado-Maso - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b

Bai Gaeshi - D, DF, F, x or y

Sabaki no Aikuchi - F, D, DF, x or y

Bakudan Pachiki - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b

Yakiire - D, DF, F, a

Sunakake - D, DF, F, b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Guillotine - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Drill - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Guillotine - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Drill - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y


*Fatal Fury Mode(select to start + any key)

-- Throw
Shime Age - F or B, y

Bun Nage - F or B, b


-- Command Moves
Bussashi - F, x


-- Super Moves
Hebi Tsukai: Upper - D, DB, B, x

Hebi Tsukai: Middle - D, DB, B, a

Hebi Tsukai: Lower - D, DB, B, y

Sado-Maso - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b

Bai Gaeshi - D, DF, F, x or y

Sabaki no Aikuchi - F, D, DF, x or y

Bakudan Pachiki - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b

Yakiire - D, DF, F, a or b

Todome - (opponent grounded)D, D, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Guillotine - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Drill  - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y
 ->Lv5 - (during Lv4)x+a+y

-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Guillotine - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Drill  - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y
 ->Lv5 - (during Lv4)x+a+y
