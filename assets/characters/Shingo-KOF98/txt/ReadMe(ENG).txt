=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Hatsu Gane - F, y

Issetsu Seoi Nage (Incomplete) - F, b


-- Command Moves
Ge Shiki: Goufu You (in form only) - F, a


-- Super Moves
114 Shiki: Ara Gami (Incomplete) - D, DF, F, x

115 Shiki: Doku Gami (Incomplete) - D, DF, F, x

100 Shiki: Oni Yaki (Incomplete) - F, D, DF, x or y

101 Shiki: Oboro Guruma (Incomplete) - B, D, DB, a or b

Shingo Kick - B, DB, D, DF, F, a or b

Shingo Kinsei Ore Shiki: Nie Togi - F, D, DF, a or b

Elbow Drop (tentative) - D, DB, B, x or y


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Ge Shiki: Kake Hourin - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Barning Shingo - D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)

Barning Shingo - D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y
