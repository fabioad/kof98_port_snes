Sakura Kasugano(KOF'98 Style) by Ikaruga
for WinMugen / Mugen 1.0

*Open Source

=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Sakura Zime - F, y

Sailor Shoot - F, b


-- Command Moves
Flower Kick - F, a

Guts Kick - DB, b


-- Super Moves
Hadou Ken - D, DF, F, x or y

Shouou Ken - F, D, DF, x or y

Shunpu Kyaku - D, DB, B, a or b

Sakura Otoshi - F, D, DF, a or b
 ->Follow Up  - x or y(3 times)

Ouka Kyaku - (in air)D, DF, F, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Shinku Hadou Ken - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y(Hold)

Haru Ichiban - D, DB, B, D, DB, B, a or b

Midare Zakura - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b(can use in air)


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Shinku Hadou Ken - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y(Hold)

Haru Ichiban - D, DB, B, D, DB, B, a or b

Midare Zakura - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b

Shungoku Satsu - x, x, F, a, y
