=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


-- Throw
Saka Hagi - F, y

Saka Saka Hagi - F, b


-- Command Moves
Ge Shiki: Yume Biki - F, x -> x

Ge Shiki: Goufu In "Shini Gami" - F, a

Ge Shiki: Yuri Ori - (in air)B, a


-- Super Moves
108 Shiki: Yami Barai - D, DF, F, x or y

100 Shiki: Oni Yaki - F, D, DF, x or y

127 Shiki: Aoi Hana - D, DB, B, x or y(3 times)

212 Shiki: Kototsuki In - F, DF, D, DB, B, a or b

Kuzu Kaze - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, x or y

311 Shiki: Tsuma Kushi - F, D, DF, x or y


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Kin 1211 Shiki: Ya Otome - D, DF, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y

Ura 108 Shiki: Ya Sakazuki - D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Kin 1211 Shiki: Ya Otome - D, DF, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y

Ura 108 Shiki: Ya Sakazuki - D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F, x or y

Ura 311 Shiki: Saku Tsuma Gushi - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b
