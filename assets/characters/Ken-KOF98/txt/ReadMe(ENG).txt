for WinMugen / Mugen 1.0

=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


-- Throw
Seoi Nage - F, y

Jigoku Guruma - F, b


-- Command Moves
Inazuma Kakato Wari - F, a

Kurubushi Kick - DB, b

Blocking(Upper) - F, x+y

Blocking(Lower) - D, a+b


-- Super Moves
Hadou Ken - D, DF, F, x or y

Shouryu Ken - F, D, DF, x or y

Tatsumaki Senpu Kyaku - D, DB, B, a or b(can use in air)

Nata Otoshi Geri(*) - B, DB, D, DF, F, a

Kama Barai Geri(*) - B, DB, D, DF, F, b

Oosoto Mawashi Geri(*) - B, DB, D, DF, F, a+b

Inazuma Kakato Wari - hold a or b while(*)


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Shouryu Reppa - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Sippu Zinrai Kyaku - D, DB, B, D, DB, B, a or b


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Shouryu Reppa - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Sippu Zinrai Kyaku - D, DB, B, D, DB, B, a or b

Sinryu Ken - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b(Tap)
