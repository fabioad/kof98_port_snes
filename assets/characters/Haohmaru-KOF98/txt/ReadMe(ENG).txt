=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


-- Throw
Kuzushi -> Hien Giri - F or B, y or b


-- Command Moves
Hiza Uchi Geri - DB, b

Suberikomi Geri - DF, b


-- Super Moves
Ougi: Senpu Retsu Zan -D, DF, F, x or y

Ougi(fake): Senpu Retsu Zan -D, DF, F, a

Ougi: Kogetsu Zan - F, D, DF, x or y

Ougi: Resshin Zan - B, D, DB, x or y

Zankou Sen - F, DF, D, DB, B, a or b


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Tenha Seiou Zan - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, x or y

Hi Ougi: Tenha Fuujin Zan - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Tenha Seiou Zan - F, DF, D, DB, B, F, x or y

Hi Ougi: Tenha Fujin Zan - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, x or y

Hi Ougi: Tenha Danku Retsu Zan - D, DF, F, D, DF, F, a or b
