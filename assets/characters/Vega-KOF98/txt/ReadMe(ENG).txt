=====<Movelist>=====

-- Button Assign
x - Light Punch
y - Hard Punch
z - Knockdown Attack
a - Light Kick
b - Hard Kick
c - Maximum Mode(ADV)/Power Charge(EX)
start - Taunt


-- Moves
Run(ADV)/Front Step(EX) - F, F
Back Step - B, B
Knockdown Attack - y+b or z
GC Knockdown Attack - y+b or z while guarding
Roll(ADV)/Evade(EX) - x+a
GC Roll - x+a while guarding(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Maximum Mode(ADV) - x+a+y(Requires 1 Power Bar)
Power Charge(EX) - x+a+y
Safe Fall Recovery - x+a when nearing ground while falling


*Normal Mode

-- Throw
Deadly Throw - F, y

Death Tower - F, b


-- Command Moves
Command Moves(1) - F, x


-- Super Moves
Psycho Crusher - (charge)B, F, x or y

Double Knee Press - (charge)B, F, a or b

Devil Reverse - (charge)D, U, x or y
 ->Follow Up  - x or y

Head Press - (charge)D, U, a or b
 ->Somersault Skull Diver - x or y

Vega Warp - F, D, DF, x+y or a+b(Forward) / B, D, DB, x+y or a+b(Back)


-- DM(ADV: Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: MAX Mode or In low life)
Mega Psycho Crusher - (charge)B, F, B, F, x or y

Knee Press Nightmare - (charge)B, F, B, F, a or b


-- SDM(ADV: In MAX Mode & Requires 1 Power Bars / EX: In MAX Mode & In low life)
Final Psycho Crusher - (charge)B, F, B, F, x or y

Knee Press Nightmare - (charge)B, F, B, F, a or b
